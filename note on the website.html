Data (simulation)

100% synthetically generated data!

None of the projects on the dashboard are real student submissions—they were created by a benchmarking script (

simulate_large_cohort.py) specifically to stress-test the mathematical funnel at full scale (450 teams $\rightarrow$ 50 finalists) before real students arrive.

The simulation generator is grounded the sample projects in the real-world regional innovation themes of Universidad Anáhuac Mayab and the Yucatán Peninsula:

  • 2do Semestre (Feria de Prototipos):

    • Bio-bloques térmicos de sargazo y cal yucateca.

    • Sensores IoT para colmenas de abeja melipona maya.

    • Desalinizadores solares para ranchos ganaderos en Tizimín.

    • Robots acuáticos autónomos para limpieza de cenotes en Homún/Cuzamá.

    • Pellets combustibles de bagazo de henequén.

  • 4to Semestre (Business Model Canvas):

    • Plataformas de visión artificial para citricultores de Oxkutzcab.

    • Redes de micro-bodegas refrigeradas para la pesca en Progreso y Celestún.

    • Microcréditos por WhatsApp para artesanas bordadoras en Maní y Teabo.

    • Vajillas compostables de hoja de plátano para hoteles de la Riviera Maya.

    • Kioscos escolares de triaje metabólico infantil en Mérida.

How it will work in Production:

In production it starts completely blank.

  1. Clean Slate at Launch:

    • The submissions database (submissions.json) starts with 0 records.

    • The table displays: “Esperando entregas de la cohorte Enero 2026…”.

  2. As Real Teams Submit:

    • As students fill out Tab 1 (Entrega y Registro) and upload their BMCs, prototype specs, and video links, their real projects populate the database with official folio numbers (e.g., ANH-2026-F1001).

  3. Execution of the 5-Judge Panel:

    • When Phase 1 screening begins, the 5 Synthetic Judges run on your dual Titan GPUs against the actual submitted text and files.

    • The Statistical Calibrator calculates the real mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$) of the incoming January cohort, normalizes scores using Z-scores ($S_{cal} = 75 + 10z$), flags borderline projects, and filters the real Top 50 Finalistas for the human jury.
