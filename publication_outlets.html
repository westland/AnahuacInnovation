Journal Outlets for Project Reporting

To maximize visibility among deans, program directors, and researchers running entrepreneurship and innovation programs in Latin America (especially Mexico), Spain, and the US, you should target journals that specialize in either Entrepreneurship/Management Education or Educational Technology / AI in Higher Education.

Below are the most strategic, high-profile journals organized by geography and editorial focus:

1. High-Impact Channels for Spain & Latin America (Spanish & Bilingual)

Publishing here will directly catch the attention of academic leaders and innovation hub directors at institutions like Tecnológico de Monterrey, UNAM, IPN, Universidad de Chile, IE University, Universidad de Navarra, and Mondragon.

  • RIED: Revista Iberoamericana de Educación a Distancia (UNED, Spain)

    • Focus: Digital transformation, AI, and scalable pedagogies in higher education.
    • Profile: Indexed in JCR / Scopus (Q1). It is arguably the most prestigious open-access journal in Ibero-America for scalable digital higher education. They frequently publish special issues on Generative AI.
    • Language: Spanish and English.

  • Comunicar (Spain)

    • Focus: Media, technology, and emerging computational interfaces in education.
    • Profile: JCR / Scopus Top 1% (Q1). Extremely high citation rates across Spain and Latin America. Excellent if you emphasize the conversational video avatar (Tavus) interface and human-AI student interaction.
    • Language: Bilingual (published concurrently in English and Spanish).

  • Innovación Educativa (IPN, Mexico)

    • Focus: Case studies and systemic pedagogical models in Latin America.
    • Profile: Indexed in SciELO, Latindex, Redalyc. Widely cited across Mexican public and private higher education ecosystems. Ideal for framing this as a solution to structural resource constraints (“X NO hay coaches”).

  • EDUTEC-e: Revista Electrónica de Tecnología Educativa (Spain)

    • Focus: Practical, applied educational technology solutions.
    • Profile: Scopus-indexed. Very popular among university educational innovation centers across Spain and the Southern Cone.

2. High-Profile Global & US Channels: Entrepreneurship & Business Education

If you want business school deans and entrepreneurship center directors (e.g., Babson, Stanford, MIT, Harvard, IE, Esade) to adopt this framework, target journals specializing in entrepreneurship pedagogy:

  • Academy of Management Learning & Education (AMLE) [AOM - US]

    • Focus: Groundbreaking structural changes in management and entrepreneurship curriculum.
    • Profile: The undisputed premier journal (FT50 list, Q1). Very rigorous. If you report a controlled study showing that 5-judge synthetic agent panels provide grading parity/fairness (Objetivo Justo) equal to or better than overwhelmed human panels, this is the gold standard target.

  • Entrepreneurship Education and Pedagogy (EE&P) [SAGE - US / Global]

    • Focus: Dedicated entirely to innovations in entrepreneurship classrooms, bootcamps, and incubators.
    • Profile: Official journal of USASBE (United States Association for Small Business and Entrepreneurship). Highly read by university incubator managers and entrepreneurship faculty.

  • Education + Training (Emerald - UK / Global)

    • Focus: The intersection of enterprise education, student employability, and new teaching methods.
    • Profile: Scopus Q1. Highly influential in the European entrepreneurship workout community.

3. High-Profile Global & US Channels: AI, Architecture & EdTech

If your article emphasizes the technical design—such as the multi-agent consensus pipeline, local open-weight inference (vLLM/DeepInfra), and real-time avatar interaction:

  • International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (ETHE) [Springer / UOC - Spain/Global]

    • Focus: Systemic educational technology interventions in universities.
    • Profile: Open Access, Scopus Q1, very high impact factor. Jointly operated out of Barcelona and Latin American partner universities; read globally. It bridges Spanish, European, and US academic audiences seamlessly.

  • Computers & Education: Artificial Intelligence (Elsevier - Global / US)

    • Focus: Purely dedicated to LLMs, autonomous agents, and AI systems in education.
    • Profile: The newest sister journal to Computers & Education. High velocity, perfectly suited for multi-agent LLM systems and synthetic judges.

  • IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies (TLT)

    • Focus: The engineering and software architecture behind educational systems.
    • Profile: Excellent if you want to publish the software pipeline, prompt engineering framework, and rubric-parsing agent orchestration.

Recommended Publication & Framing Strategy

To maximize academic prestige and cross-continental adoption, consider a two-step publication approach:

Phase Target Channel Article Angle / Framing
1. Conference Bridge (Immediate Regional Reach) IFE Conference (formerly CIIE at Tec de Monterrey, Mexico) or USASBE (US) Present this as a practice-in-progress case study: “Scaling 450 Venture Pitches with Autonomous Synthetic Judges and LLM Avatars.” Tec de Monterrey’s IFE conference is the single largest gathering of higher ed innovators in Latin America.
2. Formal Journal (High Citation & Policy Impact) ETHE (Springer/UOC) or RIED Frame the study empirically: Compare the 450 projects evaluated by human coaches vs. evaluated by the AI panel. Measure inter-rater reliability, cost/time saved, and student perception of the Tavus avatar sparring partner.
